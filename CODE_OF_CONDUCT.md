# Code of Conduct

zOS is a production-sensitive infrastructure project. Discussion and review should stay technical, respectful, evidence-based, and focused on safe outcomes.

## Expected behavior

- communicate clearly and professionally;
- critique ideas and changes rather than people;
- distinguish verified facts, assumptions, and proposals;
- disclose operational risk and uncertainty instead of hiding it;
- avoid publishing secrets, credentials, sensitive topology data, or private incident details;
- respect maintainer decisions intended to protect production safety and repository integrity.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, deliberate disclosure of private/security-sensitive information, attempts to bypass project safety controls, or knowingly misleading operational claims are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

The repository maintainer may edit, hide, lock, or remove content; close or restrict issues/PRs; or block participants when needed to protect contributors, users, or infrastructure.

Security disclosures should follow `SECURITY.md`. Support and operational questions should follow `SUPPORT.md`.
